Global ERP, CRM and banking systems for a PE exit

Siprocal (contract) · Vice President, Operations · 2021–2024

Problem A PE-backed, multi-entity digital marketing technology company growing across North and South America had no single system of record for finance and go-to-market data. That slowed exit readiness and left heavy bank reconciliation work and forecast blind spots across US and LATAM entities.

Action As ERP program leader, installed a 90-day Run-the-Business / CPFR process first, then implemented NetSuite ERP and HubSpot CRM as the global system of record with NetSuite, LatamReady and Deloitte Tax partners, integrated bank partners through Kyriba, and built a real-time forecast and KPI dashboard for leadership.

Result One global system of record supporting PE exit readiness, about 50% less banking reconciliation effort, and real-time forecast and KPI visibility for the $40M business.

Post-bankruptcy supply chain rebuild for a national retailer

Standard General (private equity), RadioShack · Vice President, Supply Chain Operations · 2015–2016

Problem A PE-owned retailer emerging from bankruptcy needed a supply chain that could support national retail without the cost and service failures of the prior model. Asian vendor, inventory and store-fulfillment complexity threatened in-stock levels and working capital.

Action Led a 150-person supply chain organization (3PL management, forecasting, demand planning, purchasing, international logistics, replenishment) managing $30M in cost and $500M+ in purchases. Ran a cost analysis and outsourced supply chain functions, redesigned store pack-ship logic for a 95%+ store in-stock design, and renegotiated contracts with about 74 Asian vendors.

Result $5M lower annual supply chain cost, a 57% improvement in key supply chain metrics (forecast accuracy, in-stock percentage, inventory turns, delivery times), and 33% faster order fulfillment.

First fully outsourced logistics program for a wireless carrier

Leap Wireless International (Cricket) · Senior Director, Operations · 2011–2013

Problem A prepaid wireless carrier serving 4.6 million subscribers had logistics that could not scale economically; warehouse-to-store accuracy and forecast quality limited growth and capital efficiency.

Action Conceived and led the first fully outsourced logistics (4PL) program for a wireless carrier, cleaned store-level SKU and minimum-order master data for the 3PL handoff, and installed a carrier S&OP rhythm (product, 4P, consensus demand).

Result $100M reduction in capital spending, warehouse-to-store order accuracy above 99%, and forecast accuracy above 75%.
